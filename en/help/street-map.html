Map and tourist route

Walk the area and hear narrations of nearby places.

When you enter, the map opens and the microphone turns on.

Legend

With the microphone on:

Max = say this first.

Command = what you ask after Max.

Field = label for a form value.

Button = tap on screen.

Start route

Tap Start route or say:

  • Max start

If you are asked, tap Yes or No, or say it (without Max).

During the route

Tap the map to see Radius, Centre and Stop route. Adjust Radius to widen or narrow the area. Tap Centre to return to your position. Tap Stop route to finish; confirm with Yes or No.

Narration

As you pass a place, you hear the narration. Use the controls to pause, resume or replay. For next, previous or keep walking, use the icons or say Max and a command:

  • Max stop
  • Max resume
  • Max repeat
  • Max next
  • Max previous
  • Max walking

Learn more

After the short narration, TourAI asks if you want to know more. Tap + or say yes or no, without Max.

Ask

With the microphone on, say Max and your question:

  • Max ask

Max help

With the microphone on, say Max and this screen's command:

  • Max help
